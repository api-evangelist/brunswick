METTAWA, Ill. – March 31, 2026 – Brunswick Corporation (NYSE: BC), the global leader in marine technology, today announced that two of its emerging leaders, Vittorio Bichucher, President of Venture Boat Group and General Manager of NAVAN Boats, and Carly Warren, Director of Member Services at Freedom Boat Club of Boston and Cape Cod, have been named to Boating Industry Magazine's 2026 40 Under 40 list. The honorees are featured in the March 2026 issue of Boating Industry. Each year, Boating Industry selects 40 outstanding young professionals from hundreds of nominations across all segments…
