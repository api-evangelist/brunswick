Latest honors from the ExSys Awards add to a record-setting year for Brunswick's CES exhibit, which earned recognition for innovation, storytelling and immersive technology experiences. METTAWA, Ill. (July 29, 2026) – Brunswick Corporation (NYSE: BC), the global leader in marine technology, today announced that its CES 2026 exhibit earned two ExSys Awards from The Experiential Design Authority (TEDA) and Exhibit City News, recognizing excellence in experiential exhibit design and execution.
