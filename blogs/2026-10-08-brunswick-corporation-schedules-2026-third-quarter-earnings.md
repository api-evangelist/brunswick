METTAWA, Ill., Oct. 08, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Brunswick Corporation (NYSE: BC) will release its 2026 third quarter financial results on Thursday, October 29, 2026, before the market opens by way of an advisory release, notifying the public that the complete and full-text results will be available on the Company’s website . The results will also be available on the SEC’s website with the Form 8-K filing of the release .
