METTAWA, Ill. (July 1, 2026) - Brunswick Corporation (NYSE:BC), the world's largest manufacturer of recreational marine products, today applauded the introduction of the Boat Loan Interest Deduction Act of 2026 (H.R. 9532) by Congressman Rudy Yakym (IN-02) and Congressman Don Davis (NC-01).
