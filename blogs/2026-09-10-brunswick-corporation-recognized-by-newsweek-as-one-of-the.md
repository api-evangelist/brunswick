METTAWA, Ill. (September 10, 2026) – Brunswick Corporation (NYSE: BC), the world’s largest marine technology company, has been named to Newsweek’s 2026 list of the World’s Most Trustworthy Companies, marking its fourth consecutive year receiving the distinction. Recognized in the Machines & Industrial Equipment category, Brunswick’s continued inclusion reflects the trust it has earned among employees, customers, and investors through its commitment to innovation, integrity, and long-term value creation.
