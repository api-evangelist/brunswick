METTAWA, Ill., April 15, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Brunswick Corporation (NYSE: BC) today confirmed that, supported by its diversified manufacturing footprint, the recent amendments to Section 232 tariffs on U.S. imports of steel, aluminum and copper are anticipated to have a de-minimis impact on Brunswick’s consolidated financial results. Brunswick will provide more information on its first quarter earnings call on Thursday, April 30 at 11:00 AM ET.
