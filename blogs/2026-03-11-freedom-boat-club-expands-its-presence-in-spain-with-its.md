AGUADULCE, Spain – March 11, 2026 – Freedom Boat Club, the world’s largest boat club and a business of Brunswick Corporation (NYSE: BC), today announced the opening of its newest location in Aguadulce, Spain. This latest expansion strengthens the Club’s presence along the Costa de Almería and furthers its mission to make boating more accessible throughout the Mediterranean. The new location follows the successful launch of Freedom Boat Club’s operations in Almerimar in 2024 and is part of a strategic plan of progressive growth within the province of Almería.
